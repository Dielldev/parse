Workflow

SQ Rrjedha e punës · SR Tok rada

Atlas answers three separate questions, each in a way that can be measured and audited: is a post threat-relevant (detection), is the story big enough (significance), and is it true (validation). Truth and spread are separate: a false story spreading fast is still alerted, marked Unverified or Debunked.

Pipeline8 stages

  1. CollectRegistry sources only
  2. ArchiveSHA-256 evidence
  3. NormaliseLanguage, script, OCR
  4. DetectCalibrated probabilities
  5. ClusterEvents, origin groups
  6. ScoreTPS + tripwires
  7. VerifyP1/P2 only
  8. AlertHuman decides

The rules

DEBUNKEDA Tier-0 source contradicts within its own scope, or the media is recycled from an earlier event.
CONFIRMEDAt least one Tier-0 source supports within its own scope, and nothing contradicts.
DISPUTEDBoth supporting and contradicting evidence exist.
CORROBORATEDAt least 2 independent origin groups at Tier ≤ 2 support it.
UNVERIFIEDAnything else. Lists what is missing as suggested checks.
