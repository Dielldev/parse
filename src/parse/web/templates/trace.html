Where did this story start, and who carried it?

When a story about Kosovo spreads, analysts find out late and cannot see where it began or who is pushing it. Give the Tracer one post, a link, a screenshot or a piece of text. It walks back through the archive to the earliest observed source, draws the path the story travelled in Albanian, Serbian and English, names who amplified it, and checks the claim against independent sources.

The problem

Stories settle before anyone answers them. There is no record of how one spread, and no way to link a repost to its original.

What the Tracer does

Typed edges between posts (forward, quote, copy, link, translate), one tree per independent origin, the earliest root as origin.

How we test it

MVP target: ≥ 70% of P1/P2 alerts show the earliest known post plus ≥ 2 spread steps. Every trace below says if it passes.

The rule

“Earliest observed source”, “amplified by”. Never “Serbia/Russia did X”: formal attribution belongs to the authorities.

If Parse already archived the page it uses that copy. Otherwise it fetches it, only from sources in the registry, and archives it first.
